Screenplay Format Guide: Courier 12pt Margins & Layout Spec

In Hollywood, scripts with improper margins get tossed into the rejection bin before page three is finished. Master standard 12pt Courier margin geometry, element indentations, and the mechanics of professional screenplay architecture.

Standard Screenplay Layout Specifications

Industry standard screenplays must be formatted in 12pt Courier font with specific margin offsets from the paper's left edge:

  • Left Page Margin: 1.5 inches (provides physical space for three-hole brad binding).
  • Right Page Margin: 1.0 inch.
  • Top & Bottom Margins: 1.0 inch (yielding approximately 54–55 lines per page).
  • Character Heading Indent: 3.7 inches from the left edge (centered block).
  • Dialogue Block Indent: 2.5 inches from the left edge (right margin at 6.0 inches).
  • Parenthetical Indent: 3.1 inches from the left edge.

Frequently Asked Questions: Screenplay Formatting

How many pages should a feature film screenplay be?

A standard feature film script runs between 90 and 110 pages. Scripts under 85 pages feel underdeveloped to studio readers, while spec scripts exceeding 120 pages are frequently rejected before coverage begins.

What is the difference between (O.S.) and (V.O.)?

(O.S.) stands for Off-Screen: The character is physically in the scene's location (such as yelling from the next room), but not visible to the camera. (V.O.) stands for Voice-Over: The character is not physically present in the room; their voice comes from narration, telephone calls, or internal monologue.

Should action blocks be justified or left-aligned?

Always left-aligned (ragged right). Never justify screenplay text, as justifying creates uneven letter-spacing that disrupts the monospaced Courier timing.
