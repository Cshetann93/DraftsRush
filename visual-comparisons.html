Case Study 01

The Cold Open Punch-In

A high retention cold open eliminates introductory pauses. The script pairs a direct thesis statement with rapid cutaways to stop viewers from scrolling away.

The Written Script

VISUAL:

Tight digital punch-in on speaker eye line. Neutral studio background.

AUDIO (VO):

Most video essays fail in the edit room.

VISUAL:

Hard cut to screen recording. Scrambled NLE timeline with red render warnings.

AUDIO (VO):

They fail right here. On page one of the draft.

Edit Room Cut Log
MARK 0:00.00 // A-ROLL PRIMARY

Framing: Digital punch to 115% scale. Center anchor lock on eye level. Audio track: Dialogue set to -14 LUFS.

MARK 0:01.85 // HARD B-ROLL CUT

Asset: 4K timeline macro capture. Overlay: 8% speed ramp on mouse glide. SFX: Mechanical switch snap.

MARK 0:03.20 // RETENTION BRIDGE

Pacing: Quick jump cut to straight A-roll at 100% standard wide framing. Audio: Background synth pulse fades in at -24dB.

Pacing: 3.20s total Cut Frequency: 1.06s
Case Study 02

The Data Proof Point

Analytical claims quickly lose viewer attention if numbers stay abstract. The script isolates a single key metric and pairs it with visual callouts on screen.

The Written Script

VISUAL:

Full-screen chart display. Highlight marker underlines the steep retention cliff at minute 1:30.

AUDIO (VO):

Fifty-four percent of your audience walks out before the second point.

VISUAL:

Cut to clean typography callout in yellow brutalist banner: "THE 90-SECOND TRAP".

AUDIO (VO):

Unless you reset their focus with a mini-arc.

Edit Room Cut Log
MARK 0:00.00 // GRAPHIC CANVAS

Graph asset keyed at 1080x1920. Red line graph traces downward across 1.40s duration. SFX: Low frequency sub drone.

MARK 0:02.10 // CALLOUT OVERLAY

Yellow card banner enters with direct 2-frame snap transition. Font: Bold grotesk caps. SFX: Paper slap transient.

MARK 0:04.00 // SCENE RE-ANCHOR

Motion blur whip pan back to speaker desk. Subject resets position to deliver the solution hook.

Pacing: 4.80s total Motion Elements: 2
Case Study 03

The Commercial B2B Demo

Product videos should avoid vague feature rundowns. The script pairs concise voiceover claims with tight macro captures of software workflows.

The Written Script

VISUAL:

Split screen: Left side shows slow manual document drafting. Right side shows clean structured layout.

AUDIO (VO):

Stop managing scripts like school essays.

VISUAL:

Left side dissolves away completely. Right side expands to full screen showing timecode and word count targets.

AUDIO (VO):

Build production assets that editors can execute without guesswork.

Edit Room Cut Log
MARK 0:00.00 // 50/50 SPLIT SCREEN

Dual window layout with 4px black dividing border. Track 1: Desaturated manual doc. Track 2: High contrast formatted sheet.

MARK 0:02.40 // FRAME EXPANSION

Left window opacity cut to 0%. Right track scales from 50% width to 100% canvas in 12 frames via ease-out curve.

MARK 0:04.10 // CALLOUT PUNCH

Highlighter box animates over target word count. SFX: Subtle camera shutter click. Music drops into full rhythm.

Pacing: 5.50s total Track Count: 4 layers
