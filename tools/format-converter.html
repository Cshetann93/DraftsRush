Why Digital Video Uses Dual-Column Formatting

Standard Screenplay vs. AV Format

Standard Courier 12pt format is designed for actors on a soundstage. But for YouTube, video essays, and commercials, the editor needs to know what visual corresponds to every exact spoken phrase. Dual-column eliminates guesswork by anchoring voiceover lines directly opposite B-roll instructions.

Syncing Sound and Sight

When an editor receives a dual-column sheet, they can read down the left side to compile stock assets, 3D renders, and text pop-ups before voiceover recordings even finish. It cuts editing turnaround time by up to 50%.
