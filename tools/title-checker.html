CTR Guidelines

A Practical Guide to Stronger YouTube Titles

What is the character limit for YouTube titles on mobile?

YouTube permits titles up to 100 characters, but visible length varies by device, placement, and text width. Treat this preview as a planning aid rather than an exact platform guarantee. Put the clearest subject and strongest promise near the beginning.

What is the ideal title length for maximum CTR?

There is no universal ideal character count or guaranteed CTR range. Aim for a title that is specific, accurate, and easy to understand at a glance. Remove filler before cutting meaningful context.

Title workshop / 01

How to improve a video title in 4 passes

Use the checker as a final review, not as a substitute for a clear idea.

  1. 01 / Name the subject

    Make it obvious what the viewer will learn, see, or experience. Replace vague wording with a concrete subject.

  2. 02 / State the payoff

    Give a credible reason to watch: a question answered, a problem solved, or a surprising finding.

  3. 03 / Remove the padding

    Cut repeated words and generic openers. Keep essential context and avoid overpromising.

  4. 04 / Check the package

    Read the title beside the thumbnail. Let them complement each other rather than repeat the same message.
