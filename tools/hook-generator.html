Free Video Hook Generator: How It Works

Is this hook generator truly 100% free?

Yes. Unlike other subscription SaaS platforms that demand credit cards or sign-ups, this generator is completely free and executes locally inside your browser. Generate unlimited hooks for your YouTube videos, Reels, and scripts with zero fees.

How does a hook stop viewers from swiping?

An unskippable hook creates an open curiosity loop within 3 seconds. By presenting an unexpected anomaly, loss-aversion warning, or counterintuitive fact under 14 words, viewers feel compelled to stay through Act 1 to discover the resolution.

What is the optimal speaking pace for a video hook?

Standard high-retention video pacing runs at approximately 140 to 160 Words Per Minute (WPM). That translates to roughly 2.5 words per second, meaning your opening sentence must land between 8 and 14 words.

Can I copy and test these hooks in other tools?

Yes. Use the one-click copy buttons above, then verify word counts and spoken seconds using our 5-Second Hook Analyzer or calculate full script durations in the Script Timer.
