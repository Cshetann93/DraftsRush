How long is my script? Formula explained

To calculate your video script duration manually, divide your total word count by your speaking rate (WPM) and multiply by 60 for seconds: (Words ÷ WPM) × 60 = Seconds. A 750-word script read at 150 WPM equals 5.0 minutes (300 seconds).

How many words is a 5-minute video?

At a balanced conversational speed of 145 WPM, a 5-minute video script requires roughly 725 words. If your cut contains heavy b-roll pauses, diagram walk-throughs, or screen recordings, target 650 words to avoid rushed speech.
