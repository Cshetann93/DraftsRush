How many words is a 10-minute video?

At a comfortable conversational speed of 145 WPM, a 10-minute video script requires roughly 1,450 words. If your video includes complex visuals, diagrams, or B-roll pauses, target 1,300 words to give the edit room to breathe.

Does speech pace change between YouTube and TikTok?

Yes. Short-form viewers expect zero dead space, pushing typical speeds to 160–180 WPM. Long-form video essays benefit from lower pacing (130 WPM), allowing viewers time to absorb complex arguments.
