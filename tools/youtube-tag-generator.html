03 / METADATA BASICS

How to Choose YouTube Video Tags That Fit

A tag generator can help brainstorm and format terms, but relevance matters more than the number of tags. Treat the output as a draft to edit—not a checklist to fill.

+ Do

  • Use the main topic and close variations.
  • Add a common alternate spelling if viewers may use it.
  • Check that each tag describes the actual video.
  • Use a clear title and description that naturally explain the topic.

− Avoid

  • Adding unrelated high-traffic keywords.
  • Repeating the same phrase in multiple forms without a reason.
  • Putting a block of tags in the video description.
  • Assuming more tags automatically mean better rankings.

04 / COMMON QUESTIONS

YouTube Tag Generator FAQs

Quick answers about tag formatting, keywords, hashtags, and what this tool can—and cannot—do.

What is a YouTube tag generator?

A YouTube tag generator creates ideas for descriptive phrases related to a video topic and formats them as a comma-separated list. Review the suggestions and keep only tags that accurately describe your video.

How do I generate keywords for a YouTube video?

Enter your video title or subject, choose a focus type, and generate suggestions. Prioritise the main topic and specific related phrases. This tool brainstorms tags; it does not provide live search-volume or keyword-difficulty data.

What is the character limit for YouTube tags?

YouTube Studio allows up to 500 characters in the tags field. The counter helps you check the length of your comma-separated list. You do not need to fill all 500 characters if you have fewer relevant tags.

Do YouTube tags help videos rank?

YouTube says tags play a minimal role in discovery. They are mainly useful for common misspellings or alternate terms. Titles, thumbnails, and descriptions have a greater role in helping viewers understand a video.

Are YouTube tags and hashtags the same?

No. Tags are added in the dedicated Tags field in YouTube Studio. Hashtags begin with the # symbol and can be placed in a video's title or description.

Can I paste generated tags directly into YouTube Studio?

Yes. Use Copy Tags for Studio, then paste the comma-separated list into the Tags field in the video's Details settings. Review the list first and remove anything irrelevant.
