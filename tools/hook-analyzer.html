Retention Principles

The Anatomy of a 5-Second Video Hook

1. The Curiosity Gap

Create an open mental loop. Tell the viewer what happened or why it matters, but withhold the specific mechanism until they invest watch time.

2. Zero Introduction Filler

Phrases like "Hey guys, welcome back" or "In this video today" waste 2.5 seconds of critical attention. Start right inside the conflict.

3. The 14-Word Threshold

At 150 WPM, a viewer hears 2.5 words per second. To deliver your opening proposition before second 00:05, your hook must stay under 14 words.
