Frequently Asked Questions: Script Templates

What is the difference between a screenplay and a two-column script?

A screenplay uses single-column centered dialogue blocks for dramatic storytelling with actors. A two-column script explicitly pairs audio narration directly against specific visual B-roll shots, making it ideal for documentary editors and multi-media production.

How many words should a 2-column video script have?

At an average conversational cadence of 145 Words Per Minute (WPM), an 8-minute video essay script will run approximately 1,150 words, while a 60-second short script should stay around 140 to 150 words.
