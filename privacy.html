Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

DraftsRush collects minimal personal data required to deliver requested services. This includes your name, email address, company or agency details, project briefs, and reference materials submitted via our contact forms or direct communications.

2. Use of Information

Your information is used strictly to fulfill deliverables, correspond regarding drafts, issue project invoices, and handle revisions. We never sell, rent, or lease client data to data brokers or third-party advertisers.

3. Confidentiality and White-Label Protection

All submitted scripts, research materials, video links, and prompts are treated as strictly confidential. For agencies and production houses operating under subcontract or white-label agreements, DraftsRush will sign and uphold standard Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

4. Data Storage and Third-Party Infrastructure

DraftsRush operates on modern hosting infrastructure provided by Vercel. Form submissions are processed securely through compliant submission endpoints (such as Formspree or equivalent secure gateways). We do not store raw credit card numbers directly on our servers.

5. Cookies, Analytics, and Similar Technologies

DraftsRush may use cookies, local storage, pixels, tags, and similar technologies to operate the website, understand traffic and usage, measure performance, and remember preferences. These technologies may process information such as your IP address, browser and device information, pages visited, referring URLs, approximate location inferred from your IP address, and interactions with the site. The technologies used depend on the services enabled on the site and your choices.

Google Tag Manager is installed on this website to manage site tags. Depending on the tags enabled, Google Analytics or other third-party services may collect usage and device information. Please consult the relevant providers' privacy documentation for details about their processing practices.

6. Advertising and Third-Party Providers

If DraftsRush displays advertising, including ads served by Google AdSense, Google and other advertising or measurement providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve, measure, limit, and personalize ads where permitted. These technologies may collect information about visits to this and other websites to provide relevant advertising, prevent fraud, measure ad performance, and understand audience activity. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites where applicable.

You can learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Google's advertising settings and controls are available at Google Ads Settings. Advertising technologies will depend on the services actually enabled on DraftsRush.

7. Your Choices and Regional Privacy Rights

Cookie and advertising choices may be available through browser settings, Google Ads Settings, or a consent-management tool presented on the site. Where required by applicable law, non-essential storage or processing will be subject to the required consent or other lawful basis. If DraftsRush serves personalized ads to users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, it will use a Google-certified consent management platform as required by Google's policies.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a privacy request, contact care@draftsrush.com. We may need to verify your request before responding.

8. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to provide requested services, meet legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods vary depending on the type of information and why it was collected. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

If you have questions about your personal data or wish to request the deletion of your brief history and project records, contact us directly through the Contact Desk or email care@draftsrush.com.
