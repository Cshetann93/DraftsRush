Before you apply

This is a discovery directory, not a recommendation or confirmation that every listing is currently active, offers a 7–45-day loan, or is linked to an RBI-registered lender. Some links open a web search where an official website could not be confidently identified. Check the named lender in the Key Facts Statement (KFS), the total APR and fees, the repayment date, and the lender–app relationship before sharing personal information. RBI registration is not an endorsement of an app.
